# HoguLink — quiet service, clear operations

Reference: [installed Airbnb design analysis](docs/design-references/airbnb-DESIGN.md). Adopt its generous spacing, 14–16 px readable body text, 48 px primary controls, photography-led discovery and clear segmented search. Retain HoguLink's own identity, defined once as tokens in `hogulink-web/src/app/globals.css` (`:root`, `--hl-*`): canvas #f7f5f0, surface #fff, tint #f3f0e9, hairline #e8e3d9, strong line #d6cfc1; ink #141b18, secondary #39423e, muted #5d6661, subtle #8a908b; deep pine #0c1f1a / pine #12302a / pine hover #1c4239; champagne #b08d57 (text #816235, tint #f5eee2). Feature sheets reference tokens, never new hex values; legacy names (`--ink`, `--green`, `--cream`, `--line`, `--brass`) are aliases. Do not reuse competitor logos or their proprietary typefaces.

Type: use `--font-sans` and `--font-display` only. They switch per locale with `:root:lang()`; Simplified Chinese must use Chinese faces (PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei / Noto Sans SC). Japanese fonts lack characters such as 铺/边/务/费 and cause single characters to render in a heavier fallback font.

2026-10-19 premium pass (see the closing section of `refinement.css`): Fresha-style structure with a quiet-luxury palette. Larger hero statement and one elevated search bar; cards show portrait, name, shop, languages, total price and one round action (descriptions only in list view); champagne marks the active tab and eyebrows; the "how it works" band is deep pine. Workspace headings are plain page titles, KPIs are not zero-padded, the desktop sidebar is sticky. Headings inherit colour so text on dark panels stays light; never set a global heading colour.

Interactive colours: use `--hl-on-pine` / `--hl-on-pine-muted` for text and links on deep pine surfaces at every breakpoint. Selected navigation keeps champagne with pine text, including hover and keyboard focus. Use `.button.inverse` for the light action on a dark card, including shop reservation and both staff task/empty states; generic primary hover must exclude it. Define foreground and background together for hover/active variants. Gold text uses `--hl-gold-700` so small labels on champagne retain at least 4.5:1 contrast. Check actual composed backgrounds, rather than reusing light-surface muted text on dark navigation.

Consumer pages: editorial serif display paired with system sans; 8/16/24/32/48/64 spacing scale; 14–24 px card corners, modest shadows only on the floating search; real totals and available slots have priority over decoration. The home hero uses deep forest, ivory text and a restrained champagne emphasis beside the existing interior photograph. Balance long display headings; preserve the explicit search and booking hierarchy on H5. Clearly label sample people and shops. Never fabricate ratings, reviews or transaction counts. Avoid washed-out text, excessive pills, artificial gradients and status inferred from decorative badges.

Desktop workspaces: warm gray canvas, white surfaces, 220 px forest business navigation with ivory selected items, readable data tables (12 px headings, 14 px values), 4 summary tiles, a selectable queue plus detailed action area. Use 34 px page headings and 14 px descriptions on wide screens. Selected queue rows also receive an inset forest marker. Primary controls are 48 px and compact controls at least 40 px. Distinguish selected, hover, pending and error states with both text and color. All new labels support Japanese, English and Simplified Chinese. Preserve existing role scopes and address privacy. CSV exports omit addresses and customer identifiers.

Responsive: at 1100 px collapse side navigation to a horizontal strip; at 760 px stack search/filter/results and detail panels. Controls remain at least 40 px high and primary controls 48 px; visible keyboard focus, no horizontal document overflow; honor reduced motion. No fake menu destinations.

Detail and care journey: show the therapist's identity, languages and visit requirements once in a forest profile card, with the desktop planner aligned beside it. The shop hero follows the forest/photography treatment; hide decorative image captions on H5 when they compete with shop information. Reservation steps, service prices and the final quote must remain consistent. Use white forms, grouped address fields, a forest total block and explicit links to address/payment/policy sections. On original orders show the purchased service and duration from the frozen quote. Care panels distinguish captured, refunded and remaining refundable amounts; display the latest request's actual status and decision. A withdrawn request must not imply shop review occurred. Preserve access to every original case, loading/error recovery, payment authorization and customer completion consent.

Partner order journey: share a forest order overview across shop dispatch and therapist tasks, showing the frozen service, JST appointment, duration, area and actual next action. Use explicit acceptance / therapist confirmation / visit / customer approval / closed stages; cancelled orders must not imply completed service. Keep dispatch in a separate warm-white panel, original-order care and private fact cooperation together, and destructive or financial exceptions in an explicit disclosure. Focused orders may fold the large queue, but its expanded mobile state must remain visible. Preserve address redaction, nominated replacement consent, adequate service duration and explicit customer approval before capture. Loading must not appear as a missing order.

2026-10-18 visual refinement: inspected the live [Fresha home](https://www.fresha.com/zh) and [Zehitomo relaxation page](https://www.zehitomo.com/lifestyle/relaxation) in the browser. Borrow the clear primary search, strong heading hierarchy and separation of tasks; keep HoguLink's palette, artwork, data and existing order rules. At 761–1100 px the global header uses two rows so English navigation never crowds the account control. Platform tablet navigation uses its existing tab strip; shop and staff navigation becomes horizontal. `hogulink-web/src/app/refinement.css` is the shared finish layer imported after feature styles. Extend its existing sections instead of appending another competing theme sheet.
